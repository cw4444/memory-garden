# 🌿 Memory Garden ✨

*An enchanted space for capturing life's tiny magical moments*

## What is this?

Remember when Instagram was just about sharing photos with friends, before it became all algorithms and influencer culture? Memory Garden brings back that cozy feeling - but just for you (and whoever you choose to invite into your garden).

It's like that Ghibli film where everything feels warm and magical, but it's an app for collecting your daily moments. No likes, no followers, no pressure to be aesthetic - just pure "here's what made me smile today" energy.

## ✨ The Vibe

- Soft Totoro-and-Howl-inspired colors that feel like a warm hug
- Floating sparkles because why not
- Grid view for when you want to admire your collection
- Timeline view for when you're feeling nostalgic
- Little calendar dates that make every moment feel special

## 🎨 Features

- **Add photos or videos** - Capture whatever sparked joy today
- **Write captions** - From deep thoughts to "found a really good pen"
- **Switch between views** - Grid for browsing, timeline for feeling things
- **Share with friends** - Invite others into your memory garden (coming soon!)

## 🌱 How to Use

This is a vibe-coded React app, which means it was made with love, tea, and conversational magic rather than 47 terminal windows and Stack Overflow.
